DQuestions

Before you preorder.

Still unsure? Write to hello@example.com. A person on the team reads every message, usually the same day.

Q.01App

Do I need the app?

No. Everything is on the screen. The app adds history, phone alerts and firmware updates if you want them.

Q.02Accuracy

How close is it to a lab instrument?

In our 30-day test, CO₂ stayed within 26 ppm of a reference analyser on average. The test notes ship in the box.

Q.03Offline

Will it work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. It keeps about a year of readings on the device and exports them as CSV over USB.

Q.04Shipping

When will my order ship?

Batch 02 ships January 12–23, 2027. You’re charged on the day it leaves, with tracking by email.

Q.05Status light

What does the orange dot mean?

CO₂ has passed your limit, 1,000 ppm by default. It goes out when the room drops back under 800 ppm.

Q.06Returns

Can I return it?

Within 30 days for any reason, with free return shipping. The warranty is two years on both models.
