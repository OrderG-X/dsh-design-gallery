02Sensors & features

Five sensors.
One number that matters.

Most monitors show everything at once. DT-01 puts the reading that needs your attention first, and keeps the rest one glance away.

02.1CO₂ · NDIR infraredFig. 02
±30ppm+3 % of reading

A real CO₂ sensor, not a guess from VOCs.

Infrared light crosses a 42 mm chamber; CO₂ absorbs a narrow band of it, and the detector counts what’s missing. DT-01 recalibrates against fresh air once a week, so the reading in year three is as honest as on day one.

03How it works

Three minutes to set up.
Then forget it.

No hub, no subscription, no app required to read it. The app is there if you want history and alerts on your phone.

Step 01

Put it where you breathe

Within a metre of your chair, away from windows and vents. The CO₂ sensor warms up in three minutes.

Step 02

Glance, don’t scroll

The big number is whatever matters most right now. Everything else sits in a quiet column beside it.

Step 03

Orange? Open a window.

Fresh air usually brings CO₂ back under the limit in ten to fifteen minutes. The dot goes out on its own.
