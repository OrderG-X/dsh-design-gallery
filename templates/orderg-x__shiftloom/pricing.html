Who counts as an active employee?

Anyone with at least one scheduled shift during the billing month. Owners, admins and managers who only build schedules are free, however many you add.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply right away and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

What happens when the trial ends?

You choose a plan or move to Starter. Nothing is deleted, and every schedule you built stays exactly where it was.

How do staff see their shifts?

In the free Shiftloom app for phones, or by text message for anyone who would rather not install anything.

Do you offer nonprofit pricing?

Registered nonprofits, schools and community clinics get 30% off Team and Business.

Where is our data stored?

In the region you pick when you set up, encrypted in transit and at rest, with daily backups kept for 30 days.
